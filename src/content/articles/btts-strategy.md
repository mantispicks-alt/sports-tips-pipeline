Both Teams To Score (BTTS) is a clean two-way market that rewards good process.

## When BTTS – Yes has value

- Both teams have a reliable scorer **and** leak goals at the back.
- Open, end-to-end fixtures with attacking full-backs.
- Combined goals-per-game against comparable opponents sits above ~2.8.

## When BTTS – No has value

- One side is defensively elite or heavily favoured.
- A struggling attack away from home.
- Low-total, cagey matchups where both teams sit deep.
