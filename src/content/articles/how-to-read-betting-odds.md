Understanding odds is the first skill every bettor needs. This guide breaks down the three formats and shows you how to turn any price into a probability.

## Decimal odds (Europe)

The most common format across Europe. **Your total return = stake × odds.** A €10 bet at 2.50 returns €25 (€15 profit + your €10 stake).

## Fractional odds (UK)

Written like `3/1`. This means you win €3 for every €1 staked. `3/1` = 4.00 in decimal.

## Implied probability

Turn odds into a percentage: **implied probability = 1 ÷ decimal odds**. Odds of 2.00 imply a 50% chance. If you think the real chance is higher than the implied probability, you have found *value* — the foundation of long-term betting.

## Key takeaway

Never bet a price without asking: *does this reflect the real probability?* That single question separates winning bettors from losing ones.
