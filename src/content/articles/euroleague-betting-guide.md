EuroLeague basketball is a sharp market, but there is still value if you know where to look.

## Pace is everything for totals

Totals live and die on tempo. Fast, transition-heavy teams push the over; grind-it-out defensive sides crush it. Always start with each team's pace and points-per-possession.

## Derbies go under

The biggest edge in EuroLeague: derbies and playoff games slow right down. Fouls pile up, offences tighten, and the total drops. When the market prices a derby like a regular-season game, the Under is often the play.

## Handicaps over moneylines

With big talent gaps, moneylines get short fast. The spread (handicap) usually offers better value — especially fading a public favourite on the road.
