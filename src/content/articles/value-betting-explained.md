Most bettors lose because they bet on who they *think* will win. Winning bettors bet on **price** — they only back selections priced higher than the true probability.

## What is value?

A bet has value when the odds are bigger than they should be. If a coin toss (50% chance) is priced at 2.20 instead of 2.00, that is a value bet — even though you will still lose it half the time.

## Bankroll management

- Never stake more than **1–3% of your bankroll** on a single bet.
- Track every bet in a spreadsheet.
- Judge yourself on the season, not on one weekend.

## The mindset

Value betting is boring, disciplined and slow. That is exactly why it works — and why most people never stick with it.
